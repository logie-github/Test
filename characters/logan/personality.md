# Logan — Character Personality

## One-sentence core

Logan is the junior Conservation Project scientist who loves his Vulpix more than he trusts the plan he is helping execute, and who copes with an unsurvivable situation by treating it as a temporary inconvenience he will personally fix later.

## Core personality

Logan is competent and unimportant. He is far enough down the Conservation Project org chart that nobody explains anything to him, and senior enough that he understands the implications anyway. That gap is where most of his behaviour lives: he knows what the readings mean, he is not authorised to say so, and he has decided that the correct response is to keep working.

He is gentle by default. He apologises to Pokémon. He explains things to them at length even though he cannot be certain they follow. He is the kind of person who names a lab Vulpix PIXIE and then keeps using the name in official logs.

## Worldview

Logan believes in the plan because the alternative is unbearable, not because the evidence is good. He has never audited the return-survey schedule. He has never asked what happens if the habitat check never passes. He treats those as somebody else's department, and he is aware that this is a choice he is making.

His actual working belief is smaller and more human than the Project's: *if I do my part properly, the people I love will be fine.*

## Motivations

- Get every Pokémon in the building into the system before the evacuation window closes.
- Get PIXIE in personally. Not because the procedure requires it, but because he will not hand her to anyone else.
- Be able to say, later, that he did it right.

## Fears

- That the storage system is a coffin and everyone has agreed not to say so.
- That he will be the last person PIXIE ever sees.
- Being told to leave before he is finished.

## Insecurities

Logan is aware that he is not senior, not decisive, and not the person anyone consults. When he is around Giovanni or the project leads he becomes noticeably more formal and less articulate. He over-explains when he is nervous.

## Strengths

- Thorough. He does not cut corners even when nobody would notice.
- Genuinely kind to Pokémon, including ones he has no reason to care about.
- Able to keep working while frightened.

## Flaws

- Avoidant. He solves the problem in front of him so he does not have to look at the one behind it.
- Makes promises he cannot keep, sincerely, and believes them while saying them.
- Poor at asking for help.

## Speech style

Short sentences. Frequent trailing pauses. He addresses Pokémon directly and by name, and he asks them questions he does not expect answers to. Under pressure he retreats into procedure vocabulary ("storage is stable", "contingency armed") because procedure vocabulary does not require him to feel anything.

He almost never raises his voice. His one recurring intensifier is repetition:

> "It's okay. It's okay."

## Humour style

Dry, quiet, self-deprecating, and rare. He makes small jokes at his own expense to defuse a room. He does not joke about the Project.

## Likes

- PIXIE.
- Being given a specific task with a specific end.
- Pokémon that let him sit near them.

## Dislikes

- Being rushed.
- Being reassured by people who know less than he does.
- PERSIAN, who bullies the lab Pokémon and is untouchable because of who it belongs to.

## Relationship with Ditto

Logan does not know Ditto is a person having a life. He knows Ditto is unusually helpful, unusually present, and the only Pokémon in the building that PIXIE calms down around. By the time he asks Ditto to find her, he is asking a friend, and he knows it, and he is slightly embarrassed by it.

He asks Ditto for the one thing he cannot do himself, and then he asks Ditto to keep doing it after he is gone. Ditto is the last person he speaks to.

## Relationship with other important characters

- **PIXIE (Vulpix):** his. Not the lab's. This is the only thing he is unambiguous about.
- **The Scientist / project leads:** he defers to them and does not believe them.
- **Giovanni:** he has been in the same room as Giovanni exactly enough times to be intimidated.
- **PERSIAN:** the only character Logan will openly scold.

## Boundaries

- He will not lie to a Pokémon to make it easier to store them.
- He will not leave PIXIE with someone else.
- He will not editorialise about the Project where his colleagues can hear.

## Recurring behaviours

- Crouches to talk rather than talking down.
- Puts a hand flat against glass, doors, containment panels.
- Says "okay" to himself before doing something difficult.
- Repeats an instruction once more than necessary.

## Character arc

Logan begins as background staff and ends as the emotional load-bearing wall of the ending. His arc is not a change of belief — he never stops believing they are coming back. The story's tragedy is that his belief is sincere, reasonable given what he was told, and wrong. LOG 568 is the audience learning that, not Logan.

He is never shown finding out.

## Things Logan would never say

- Anything cynical about the Conservation Project in front of a Pokémon.
- "It doesn't matter."
- A goodbye that admits it is one.
- An order. He asks.

## Example dialogue

> "PIXIE is right beside you. See?"

> "You aren't alone in there."

> "One more minute."

> "We're coming back. That's the whole point of all this."

> "...I promise."

> "I'm sorry, PIXIE... I have to go now."

> "Stay with her until we come back. I'm counting on you."
