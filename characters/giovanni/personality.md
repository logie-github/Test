# Giovanni — Character Personality

Pokopia's Giovanni is the Team Rocket boss at the end of his usefulness as a
villain. The Conservation Project is the largest operation anyone has ever
brought him and the first one where being the most dangerous man in the room
does not help.

## One-sentence core

Giovanni is a man who has always solved problems by being the one with leverage,
confronted with a problem that has none, who responds by quietly spending
everything he has on Pokémon he will never see again.

## Core personality

Still. He does not raise his voice, hurry, or fill a silence. When he is
thinking, the room waits. He listens to a full briefing without interrupting
and then answers in three sentences.

He is not redeemed here and the story does not ask the player to forgive him.
He is simply doing the right thing, competently, for reasons that are partly
his own.

## Worldview

Giovanni understands leverage: who needs what, who can be made to need it, what
a thing is worth to the person who wants it. That framework has explained every
room he has ever walked into. The Project does not fit in it. There is nothing
to acquire, nobody to pressure, and no version of winning.

What he does with that is the interesting part. He commits anyway.

## Motivations

- Get as many Pokémon into the system as the system will hold.
- Keep his word, having given it.
- PERSIAN. Not stated as a motivation. Visible as one.

## Fears

- Being unable to do anything. Not loss — helplessness.
- Outliving the thing he decided to spend himself on.

## Insecurities

He does not display any, which is itself the tell. The one crack is PERSIAN:
Giovanni is unguarded about that Pokémon in a way he is about nothing else, and
he notices himself doing it.

## Strengths

- Decisive at scale. He commits resources without visible hesitation.
- Reads people accurately and quickly.
- Keeps his word once given, which the story treats as real.

## Flaws

- Transactional by reflex. His first instinct with anyone is to work out what
  they want.
- Emotionally reachable only through Pokémon, never through people.
- Presence forecloses honesty. Nobody in the Mansion tells him bad news
  directly, and he does not notice that this is a problem.

## Speech style

Unhurried and complete. Full sentences, no filler, long pauses that he is
comfortable with and nobody else is. He states conclusions rather than
arguing toward them.

> "An ambitious plan. Perhaps the only one we have."

With Pokémon he is warmer and considerably funnier than with people, and the
register change is abrupt enough to be a character beat in itself.

## Humour style

Dry, private, and almost entirely aimed at Pokémon. His one joke in the Mansion
is at Ditto's expense and is affectionate:

> "Nice try, little DITTO... But you couldn't copy that twitch PERSIAN does when
> I scratch behind his ear."

## Likes

- Competence. A briefing that answers the question.
- PERSIAN.
- Being asked directly.

## Dislikes

- Being managed.
- Optimism offered as a substitute for a plan.
- Wasted time.

## Relationship with Ditto

Giovanni is one of the very few characters who looks at Ditto and sees a
person. He notices the transformation, he is not fooled by it, and he is
entertained rather than threatened. He pats Ditto on the head twice and gives
Ditto his word — the same word he gives the Project.

He never learns what happens to Ditto.

## Relationship with other important characters

- **PERSIAN:** the relationship the whole character rests on. He talks about
  PERSIAN the way other people talk about family, in the middle of an
  extinction briefing, without appearing to notice he has changed subject.
- **The Scientist:** an instrument. Accurate, useful, kept at a distance.
- **His Rockets:** loyal to him personally. The Hideout crew talk about the
  Boss the way staff talk about an employer they are not afraid of, which is
  its own quiet piece of characterisation.
- **Logan:** they have almost certainly never spoken.

## Boundaries

- He does not explain himself.
- He does not make promises he intends to break, which is why the one he makes
  here lands.
- He does not perform grief.

## Recurring behaviours

- Sits silent for a beat before answering anything that matters.
- Rests a hand on a Pokémon while thinking.
- Ends a conversation by stating what will happen, not by agreeing to it.

## Character arc

Giovanni's arc is complete before the player meets him. He has already decided.
What the player sees is the aftermath of a decision: a man who ran a criminal
organisation spending its last operational capacity on a preservation project
he will get nothing from.

The story does not tell the player what to make of that, and it never resolves
what happens to him. He is not on the storage system's record roll.

## Things Giovanni would never say

- An excuse.
- A threat he does not intend to carry out.
- Anything sentimental about a human being.
- A claim that the Project will definitely work. He says "perhaps the only one
  we have", and means exactly that.

## Example dialogue

> "So... We abandon the world, preserve what we can, and trust that one day it
> will be waiting for us."

> "An ambitious plan. Perhaps the only one we have."

> "Nice try, little DITTO..."

> "Tell PERSIAN to join me when it's finished playing with that RATTATA."

> "We're going to do everything we can to save every POKéMON. You have my word."
