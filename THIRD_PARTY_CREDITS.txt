Third-party sprite attribution

Pokémon overworld/follower source sprites used by this mod are derived from:

Pokemon Stadium 2 Overworld Models - Gold/Silver (Gen 2)
Mod ID: STADIUM2_OVERWORLD_MODELS
Version used: 0.1.85
Author / repository credit: randyadr
Repository: randyadr/3D-Pokemon-Sprites-Gen2

Pokopia uses the NORMAL, non-shiny followsprite variants from that package.
The exact source PNGs used are included under:
THIRD_PARTY/STADIUM2_OVERWORLD_MODELS/normal_followsprites/

The original package metadata and available license/notice files are included
alongside them in THIRD_PARTY/STADIUM2_OVERWORLD_MODELS/.

The source package itself notes that some bundled third-party assets may remain
under their own licenses; its original notices are preserved here rather than
being replaced by this attribution.

Runtime sprite note for v1.0.210:
Pokopia now uses the exact files from
assets/generated/followsprites_runtime/<dex>-normal.png
in STADIUM2_OVERWORLD_MODELS v0.1.85, copied byte-for-byte into
assets/enhanced_overworld/runtime_normal/. No sprite conversion is applied.
