# Third-party credits

## Gen2-3D-Sprites follower artwork

Pokopia bundles selected **non-shiny** overworld follower sprite sheets from:

- Project: `randyadr/Gen2-3D-Sprites`
- Source: https://github.com/randyadr/Gen2-3D-Sprites
- Source directory: `assets/enhanced_overworld/poke_followers/`
- Included variant: `follower_###_normal.png` only; no `*_shiny.png` files are bundled.
- Included species are the Pokémon currently used as Pokopia overworld actors/player forms.

The source repository's `DRAMALESS_LICENSE.txt` is reproduced verbatim at
`THIRD_PARTY_LICENSES/Gen2-3D-Sprites_DRAMALESS_LICENSE.txt` so its copyright,
MIT permission notice, and additional upstream notices travel with this mod.

No submerged or shiny follower variants are included.
